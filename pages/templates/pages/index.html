Frank Moth is an artist duo based in Veria, Greece.

Selected clients include The New York Times, Adobe, Mr.Porter, Estrella world street food, Lucas Film ltd. Featured in many art & design publications worldwide like Buzzfeed, Colossal, Huffington Post and Contemporary art Curator.

The artists explore universal concepts of love, humility, truth, nostalgia and forgiveness in colorful, surreal floral portraits and futuristic retro compositions.
